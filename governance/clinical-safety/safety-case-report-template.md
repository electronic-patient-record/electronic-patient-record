# Clinical safety case report (template)

**An empty template. No report has been written, reviewed or signed.** It exists so the shape is agreed before there is
anything clinical to report on. Do not fill it in on behalf of a Clinical Safety Officer.

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| 1. Scope | System, version, intended use, what is explicitly out of scope. |
| 2. Clinical risk management summary | Link to the [plan](risk-management-plan.md) and the [hazard log](hazard-log.csv) at the release commit. |
| 3. Hazards and their status | Counts by status; every `open` hazard with its residual risk in plain words. |
| 4. Evidence | The tests, reviews and trials behind each control, by hazard id. |
| 5. Known limitations | What the product does not do, copied from the changelog's "not done" lines. |
| 6. Residual risk statement | A statement by the CSO that the residual risk is or is not acceptable for the stated use. |
| 7. Sign-off | Name, role, date, and the commit this applies to. |
