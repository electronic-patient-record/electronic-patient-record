# Refusing treatment: what was read, by place

Read 2026-11-25 for [a patient's refusal of consent](index.md). **Not legal advice, not complete, and reviewed by nobody. A pack is not made from this page without a lawyer of that place.** **P** read in the official text; **P-copy** the text on another host or an older version; **S** a summary or a search extract; **NE** not established. Section numbers are the statute's. Where a site refused automated reading it was left alone.

## The differences that decide the settings

| Question | Answers found |
|---|---|
| Does a capable adult's refusal hold when life or limb is at stake? | **Yes, in words:** Scotland (P), Northern Ireland (P, 2003 guide), Ireland (P), British Columbia (P), New South Wales (P, manual), Italy (P), South Africa (P, guidance), England's public guidance (P). **Yes, with a process against it:** Israel, where an ethics committee may approve treatment over it (P). **Partly:** France, where of three transfusions over a refusal the first two, in an unforeseen emergency, were no fault and the third was (P, 2025). **The sources conflict:** Brazil, Saudi Arabia. **Not established in a statute:** India, China, Japan, South Korea, UAE, Germany in those words, Poland, Sweden, Mexico, Texas, Queensland |
| For someone who cannot decide, does emergency treatment yield to a refusal already known? | **Yes, in the text:** Ontario, British Columbia, Victoria, Queensland (a directive), New South Wales (manual), Ireland, Northern Ireland (guide), Germany, Italy (where it can be ascertained), South Africa, Mexico (an advance document), Brazil (the 2026 Act), Israel s.15(1). **Not said or not found:** the rest |
| Is an advance refusal binding? | Binding with a form: England and Wales, Ireland, British Columbia, Victoria (instructional), Queensland (only in listed conditions), Germany, Italy, Switzerland (with doubts excepted), New York, Texas, California. Any form, at 16: Ontario. Binding without a form: Northern Ireland (common law), New South Wales. **Only for the dying or the terminal:** India, Shenzhen, South Korea, Singapore, Israel, Mexico in part. **Weighed, not binding:** Japan; Scotland ("may, in some circumstances, have legal effect"). **Not found:** Sweden, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Poland |
| How is it withdrawn? | Aloud, at any time: Germany, California, Texas (whatever the state of mind), New York, Ontario ("later wishes"), England and Wales (with capacity). Aloud with witnesses or to a doctor: Italy in an emergency, Israel, Singapore. **In writing only, by the text read:** Ireland, Victoria, Queensland, Spain, India, Mexico (the same formalities). Not established: Scotland, New South Wales, Switzerland, Brazil |
| When there is doubt | Life is preferred: Ireland, Scotland, Strasbourg, Israel. "Shall not give effect": India. The board or a court is asked: Ontario, England and Wales, Northern Ireland, with life kept meanwhile |
| From what age? | 16: Scotland, Ontario (wishes), New Zealand, Northern Ireland to consent, Netherlands, Spain. 18: England and Wales (advance decisions), Ireland, most others. 19: South Korea, British Columbia (emergency wishes). 21: Singapore. 17: Israel. 12 with maturity: South Africa to consent. No fixed age: Ontario (consent), Sweden, Italy, Germany. **Nearly everywhere someone may override a minor's refusal of life-saving care, and who differs** (a court, parents, a minister, an ethics committee, a child-protection centre) |
| Pregnancy | Texas may not withhold life support from a pregnant person (P). Northern Ireland: a refusal holds "whatever the stage of the pregnancy" (P, guide). Ireland: only while the High Court is asked (P). Elsewhere not found |
| Does psychiatric detention allow treating an unrelated physical emergency over a refusal? | No, in terms: Northern Ireland's guide, England's guidance. **Yes in part: New South Wales** (surgery in an emergency; non-surgical treatment by s.84). India's emergency section covers any treatment. **Elsewhere not established** |
| What must be written down | The refusal and that risks were explained, with an effort for a written one: US emergency rule. In writing: Spain; signed, two languages, 30 days: Saudi guidelines; signed and witnessed discharge: Mexico. Written, two witnesses "preferably": Brazil. That care was declined: Sweden. Acceptance, withdrawal and refusal noted: Italy |

## By place

| Place | Capable adult, emergency | Cannot decide, emergency | Advance refusal | Under age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| England and Wales | Holds (P, public guidance) | Best interests, MCA s.4 to 5; s.26(5) while a court is asked (P) | Binding, 18+; life-sustaining: written, signed, witnessed, "even if life is at risk" (P) | Court may allow treatment of a competent young person (S) |
| Scotland | Holds (P) | Certificate, s.47; force only if "immediately necessary" (P) | No statute; may have legal effect (P) | Adult at 16; below by understanding; override NE or doubtful (S) |
| Northern Ireland | Holds (P, 2003 guide, under review) | Necessity, unless a valid advance refusal applies (P) | Common law, binding; the 2016 Act's section is not in force (P) | Over-ruled by a parent or a court (P) |
| Ireland | Holds (P) | If no valid advance refusal is known (P) | Binding, 18+, written, two witnesses; revoked in writing; doubt favours life (P) | 16+ consent; a 16 to 17 refusal not binding until 18 (P, superseded policy) |
| Ontario | No treatment without consent (P) | s.25; not against a known wish, s.26 (P) | Any form, 16+, later wishes prevail (P) | No age; capacity (P). Court override NE |
| British Columbia | Holds (P) | Yields to a wish known at 19+ (P) | Written, two witnesses (P) | Only if in best interests (P) |
| New South Wales | Holds (P, manual) | Not against a valid prior refusal (P) | No form; "cannot be overridden in an emergency" (P) | Court may override; parents may, by the manual (P) |
| Victoria | Barred if a refusal is known (P) | No duty to search for a directive not at hand (P) | Instructional binds; two witnesses, one a doctor (P) | Blood despite refusal: two doctors (P) |
| Queensland | NE | Barred if a directive is known (P) | Adult, written, witness and certificate; life-sustaining only in listed conditions (P) | Blood despite refusal, in statute (P) |
| New Zealand | Right to refuse (P) | Best interests with views (P) | Written or oral; common law (P) | 16 as an adult (S) |
| United States | Liberty interest inferred, Cruzan (P); state law | State law | Hospital must comply with directives (P); state forms | State law |
| New York | NE | Any age, words name parents and guardians (P) | Proxy, two witnesses; revoked aloud; orders may be disregarded in listed cases (P) | A capable minor's consent also needed to withhold (P) |
| California (2017 copies) | Non-emergency cases (S) | NE | Oral or written; revoked in any manner (S) | NE |
| Texas | NE | Unconscious and life-threatening (P) | Two witnesses or notary; revoked aloud, any state of mind; not in pregnancy (P) | Minors only if parents not present (P) |
| Brazil | **Conflict:** 2026 Act and the top court for refusal; council rule 2.232/2019 art.11 for treatment; undecided (P) | Directives respected even then (P) | Written; "must be respected" (P) | A minor's refusal not accepted at relevant risk (P) |
| Mexico | Ambiguous text (P) | Treat if no advance document; report to committees and judge (P) | Terminal; two witnesses; revoked with the same formalities (P) | State must intervene where life is at risk (P) |
| South Africa | Holds (P, guidance) | If the person "has not expressly, impliedly or by conduct refused" (P, scan) | "Must respect" and "taken into consideration", both in guidance (P) | Minister may override parent or child (P) |
| Germany | General rule of consent (P) | Presumed will (P) | Written, adult; binds; revoked informally (P) | No statute; case law inconsistent (S) |
| France | Partly, above (P) | Consult the person of trust, save urgency (P, quoted) | Binds, except in a vital emergency for the time to assess (P, quoted) | Indispensable care over a parental refusal (S) |
| Spain | Right to refuse, in writing, save by law (P) | If authorisation cannot be had (P) | Written; revoked leaving a written record (P) | From 16; grave risk: representative (P) |
| Italy | Holds (P) | Respecting the will where it can be found (P) | Deed or handed in; emergency oral revocation, two witnesses (P) | No age; dispute to a judge (P) |
| Netherlands | NE | Prevent manifest serious harm (S) | Written, 16+, followed in principle (S) | 12 to 15 double consent; 16+ alone (S) |
| Switzerland (copies) | NE | Presumed will (P-copy) | Written, dated, signed; doubts excepted (P-copy) | NE |
| Sweden | No care without consent save by law (P) | Acute danger where will cannot be found (P) | No statute found | By age and maturity (P) |
| Poland | Right to refuse (P) | If the representative cannot be reached (P) | NE | 16+ double consent; court (P) |
| Council of Europe | Pindo Mulla (2024): may refuse though fatal; must be known, clear and current; efforts to dispel doubt; **failing that, protecting life should prevail**; a system once made must work (P-copy) | Art. 8 (P-copy) | Taken into account, art. 9 (P-copy) | |
| India | NE | Necessity for temporary incapacity (P) | Terminal only; notary; boards; revoked in writing (P) | NE |
| China | NE | Approval of the institution's head (P) | Shenzhen only, end stage (P) | NE |
| Japan | Guideline: no blood to the end, or transfer (P) | Doctor, on respect for life; team after (P) | Not binding (P) | 15 to 17 and under 15 rules; child centre (P) |
| South Korea | NE | Excused if incapable or delay endangers life (P) | Imminent death only, 19+, registered (P, 2018 text) | Parents and two doctors, end of life (P) |
| Singapore | Respected, dicta (P) | Best interests if no clear refusal (P) | Terminal only, 21+, registered; orally to a doctor (P-copy) | Under 21 parents (S) |
| Saudi Arabia | **Conflict** within the guidelines (P) | Must treat (P) | NE | 18 (P) |
| UAE | Free-zone policy: may refuse (P); federal NE | Where consent cannot be had "for any reason" (P, 2016 text) | None found (P) | 18 (P) |
| Israel | Ethics committee may approve over it, s.15(2) (P) | Not if known to object, s.15(1) (P) | The dying, 17+, two witnesses, 5 years; oral revocation to live (P-copy) | Parents, committee, court (P, P-copy) |
