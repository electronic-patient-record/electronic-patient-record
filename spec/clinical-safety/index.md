# Clinical safety

DCB0129-shaped safety file: clinical risk management plan, hazard log (`governance/clinical-safety/hazard-log.csv`), safety case report, named Clinical Safety Officer (OQ-2), release sign-off, SAFER Guides mapping (AS-1, AS-3). CI will fail a PR labelled `clinical-impact` that does not touch the hazard log. Hazards already known: wrong-patient overlay on merge; missed abnormal result; alert override fatigue; AI-drafted note signed unreviewed; stale downtime snapshot; terminology version drift.

**Built today (2026-10-21):** a [hazard log](../../governance/clinical-safety/hazard-log.csv) of 15 hazards for what exists (identity linking, duplicates, consent, access, audit, ingestion), a [risk management plan](../../governance/clinical-safety/risk-management-plan.md) and [safety case report](../../governance/clinical-safety/safety-case-report-template.md) as unsigned drafts, `scripts/check-hazard-log.py` (shape, score arithmetic, evidence paths exist, no `accepted`/`closed` without a named reviewer) and `scripts/check-clinical-impact.sh` (a `clinical-impact` PR must change the log). **Not built:** a named Clinical Safety Officer (OQ-2), any review of the ratings (they are the author's proposals), a safety case report, SAFER Guides mapping (AS-3). The workflow job has never run on GitHub (no remote). Otherwise planned. Source: [`../../plan.md`](../../plan.md).

Who may remove a hazard, and the evidence they must give: [`../hazard/index.md`](../hazard/index.md).
