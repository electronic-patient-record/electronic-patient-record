How to suggest an idea for Electronic Patient Record

This category is for proposing a change or a feature before anyone writes code. A good idea starts with the problem it solves, because a problem can be judged even when the first solution is not the right one.

This is a patient record, so an idea can change what a clinician sees, decides, or signs. That is fine to propose, and it is why an idea here needs a problem, a reason, and a careful look at what could go wrong.

## How to suggest an idea

Start a new discussion in Ideas, one idea per discussion. Title it with the problem or the outcome, such as "Show when a result was last reviewed", not the solution. Copy these headings into the body:

### Problem

What hurts today, for whom, and when? One or two sentences.

### Proposal

What you would change, in plain words. A short list is fine if it has parts.

### Alternatives

Other ways to solve it, including workarounds and doing nothing.

### Costs and risks

What it would make harder: complexity, compatibility, maintenance, privacy, or patient safety. If it could show a clinician something wrong, or hide something they need, say so.

### Open questions

What you are not sure about, and the one thing you most want readers to answer.

## What happens next

An idea that is taken up becomes an entry in [tasks.md](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/tasks.md) and a change to [the specification](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/spec/index.md) before any code is written, as the [contributing guide](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains. A suggestion is not a promise that it will be built, and with one maintainer, a reply may take a few days.

Bugs belong in [Issues](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/issues), and security problems belong in a private report, as [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/electronic-patient-record/electronic-patient-record/blob/main/SECURITY.md) explains.

**To start:** if you have an idea now, post it with the headings above. If you are not sure the problem is real, ask in General first.
