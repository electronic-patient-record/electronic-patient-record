Say hello: what brings you to an open-source patient record?

Welcome to General, the place for conversation that does not fit anywhere else: introductions, use cases, opinions, and questions about where the project is going.

I'll go first. I'm Joel, the project's maintainer. Electronic Patient Record aims to be a patient record that is safe by design: the clinical record is append-only, so nothing is overwritten; access is audited; and when the software compares two patient records, it explains its answer. It is an early scaffold and not for clinical use, so this is a good time to shape it.

**My one question for you:** what brought you here, and what is your connection to patient records? You might be a clinician, a developer, an integrator, a patient, a safety or privacy officer, or just curious. A sentence or two is plenty.
